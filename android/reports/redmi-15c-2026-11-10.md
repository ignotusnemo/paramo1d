# Redmi 15C device check, 10 November 2026

A real NOID mainnet payment was authorized on the Redmi, delivered to a node and included at block 297518. Its inclusion receipt was independently verified on the phone against State 297532. The same authorization packet completed an encrypted Bluetooth round trip and passed native verification on both devices with the phone's relay and RPC settings empty.

## Devices and software

- Redmi 15C, 4 GB RAM, Android 15, ARM64 with NEON and PMULL.
- Linux computer with a BlueZ RFCOMM peer using a separate unfunded test identity.
- Paramo1d 0.1.0 development APK. The wallet identity remained in Android Keystore protected storage across APK updates and process restarts.
- Parano1d verification source pinned at `848209d5db75137483cb41a94b310ad11463473f` with the released mainnet parameters.
- Measured development APK SHA-256: `bdbda5d38ab79f1d4322c87a4527dc0f49e3f0f52e8bb7cb90b8f41f4744f156`.

## Bluetooth

The phone connected to the Linux peer through authenticated, encrypted RFCOMM frames. Text messages passed in both directions. A message queued during a disconnect arrived after reconnection.

For the payment check, both relay and chain RPC settings were cleared and the protected connection was off. The phone sent the already-authorized packet to the test contact. The peer verified its Owner proof locally, encrypted the same packet to the phone and returned it over Bluetooth. The phone verified the authorization before storing and acknowledging the message. The saved card has both `via_nearby=true` and `payment.authorized=true`.

The exchange preserved the approved transaction ID. It created no second spend authorization. The inclusion receipt was subsequently checked through the independent State verifier.

A separate unfunded fixture exercised saved-view restoration in fresh native processes on the Redmi. The view was first established through full mainnet proof and slot-membership verification. ## Connections and interface

The production relay is `https://chat.parano1d.org`. Real phone connections succeeded through AmneziaWG and through the authenticated HTTPS fallback. The phone had its other VPN disabled for these checks. When another working VPN already provides connectivity, App VPN stays off.

An encrypted Unicode direct message arrived with the screen off during a wallet check while foreground delivery was active. Outgoing messages and the authenticated inbox wait run on separate workers. The server wakes waiting inbox requests when a message is published. The global waiting-request budget is 256, with a maximum ten-second wait and bounded reply size.

The Connections indicator has distinct online, offline, nearby and connecting colors. Browser checks exercised all four in both themes. The actual Redmi showed the blue nearby indicator in dark mode. The Bluetooth payment round trip completed after that fix.
